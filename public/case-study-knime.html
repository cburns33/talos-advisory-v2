What This Demonstrates

Frameworks and Thinking

01
Concentric Circle Engagement Model

Warm-to-cold audience rollout: activate CRM matched lists and open-source registrants first, then expand to TAL lookalikes, then scale with cold acquisition. This compounds efficiency rather than burning budget on cold audiences from day one.

02
SAL-First Measurement Philosophy

MQLs serve as leading indicators only. Success is measured on Sales Accepted Leads (SALs), pipeline contribution, and CAC efficiency. This aligns paid media KPIs directly to revenue, not vanity form fills.

03
Persona-Based Targeting Architecture

Four distinct buyer personas, each with specific channel behavior, content hooks, and conversion triggers: the Analytics Manager (search-led), Operations VP (LinkedIn + trade press), Enterprise Architect (competitive displacement), and Economic Buyer (championed).

04
Signal Quality Over Volume

The campaign assessment identified noise in the existing program: LinkedIn audience expansion cherry-picking easy converters, third-party syndication generating low-quality leads, and branded keyword spend distorting Google CPC benchmarks. Fix signal before scaling spend.
