01 — The Challenge

From system of record to intelligence engine.

IFS, an established leader in industrial ERP, needed to launch Nexus Black — a premium AI-driven offshoot designed to power the "autonomous enterprise." The goal was not just to generate leads, but to fundamentally shift market perception.

The challenge: how do you get a brand known for stability and reliability to be seen as the frontier of AI innovation — without alienating the existing customer base that made it successful?

"The goal was not just to generate leads, but to shift market perception of the parent brand from a 'system of record' to an 'intelligence engine.'"

— Campaign Brief, Nexus Black Launch

02 — Strategic Framework

The Concentric Circle Rollout.

To maximize budget efficiency and brand trust, the campaign was executed in three distinct phases — moving from the highest-trust audience outward to the broad market.

01
Phase 1

The Advocacy Layer

We targeted the existing IFS customer base first using first-party CRM data and LinkedIn Matched Audiences. By reaching users already familiar with the ecosystem, we created a "land and expand" effect — generating initial brand buzz from people with existing brand affinity.

02
Phase 2

The Contextual Layer

Moving beyond persona-based targeting (which relies on unreliable third-party data), we focused on Contextual Placement — securing premium inventory on technical and industrial-AI focused domains. The goal: appear as a natural participant in conversations already happening.

03
Phase 3

The Broad Market

Only after establishing trust in the first two circles did we launch broader awareness. High-integrity placements — avoiding junk display networks and mobile apps — drove the narrative to the wider enterprise C-Suite with credibility already in hand.

03 — Channel Allocation & Creative Philosophy

Budget with intention. Creative with purpose.

Budget Guardrails

Upper Funnel — Brand Storytelling 40%
Mid Funnel — Educational Capture 60%

40% of the budget was dedicated to upper-funnel brand storytelling — high-production video and thought leadership — while 60% captured the demand those brand ads generated through educational blueprints and direct response.

Creative Direction

What the user becomes — not what the AI does.

We moved away from "Product Specification" messaging and toward "Strategic Empowerment." Creative assets featured the concept of "Autonomous Flow" — clean, minimalist visuals that deliberately contrasted with the noisy, feature-heavy creative of competitors.

Autonomous Flow — Clean, Minimalist, Empowerment-Led

04 — Measuring Success

Proving it wasn't vanity spend.

Brand marketing without accountability is just expensive guessing. We built three measurement pillars that satisfied leadership and proved the investment was working.

01

Branded Search Lift

An increase in searches for "IFS Nexus" and "Nexus Black" during the launch period — proving the ads triggered active interest, not passive impressions.

02

Assisted Conversion Volume

Tracking how many "Business Hub" trial sign-ups had a Nexus Black brand ad as their first-touch attribution point — connecting brand spend to pipeline.

03

Sentiment Shift

Social listening and post-campaign surveys measured the change in the brand's association with "Innovation" vs. "Legacy" — the core perception goal of the campaign.
