# Talos Landing Page V2 Glossary

## Sections

- **Hero Section**: Top section with the main headline (`Marketing for Humans. By a Human. With AI.`) and the top CTA reveal pattern.
- **Snapshot Strip**: The section directly below the hero containing three summary cards.
- **How I Work Section**: Three-step process section (`Audit`, `Blueprint`, `Execution`).
- **Problem Section**: Narrative section explaining the core issue (includes the `Dark Funnel` term + tooltip).
- **Capabilities Section**: Core services section using neo-brutalist flip cards.
- **Logos Section**: `Brands I've Helped Build` section with client logos.
- **Final CTA Section**: Bottom conversion section with CTA reveal pattern.

## Shared Components / Elements

- **Snapshot Cards**: The three boxes in the Snapshot Strip (`Lead Gen Focus`, `Approach`, `Operating Model`).
- **Hero CTA Button**: Top neo-brutalist button labeled `Stop wasting budget`.
- **Hero Lead Form**: Form revealed after clicking the Hero CTA button.
- **Final CTA Button**: Bottom neo-brutalist button labeled `Stop wasting budget`.
- **Final Lead Form**: Form revealed after clicking the Final CTA button.
- **Flip Cards**: Individual cards in Capabilities Section that rotate on click to reveal back content.
- **Section Title**: Main heading style used at the top of each section.
- **Section Subtitle**: Supporting paragraph text under a section title.

## Naming Preference

When discussing edits, use these exact terms to avoid ambiguity:
- `Snapshot Cards` (for the three top boxes)
- `Hero CTA Button` / `Hero Lead Form`
- `Final CTA Button` / `Final Lead Form`
- `Capabilities Flip Cards`
- `Logos Section`
